About this map

  • Zoom in on any neighborhood to see individual 311 cases reported there in the last 30 days.
  • Click a neighborhood to zoom to it directly.
  • Data updates automatically once per day.
  • Pins are colored red for open cases and green for closed cases. Cases reported in the past day get a gold highlight.
  • Rates are cases per 1,000 residents. Bay Village and the Leather District aren't counted separately in population data, so they share a combined rate with the South End and Chinatown.
  • Source: Analyze Boston 311 Service Requests.
